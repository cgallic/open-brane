# Open Brane product priorities

Open Brane is actively used as its owner's everyday Brain. Improvements must make that daily use easier while preserving its general-purpose capabilities.

- Keep projects, research, notes, decisions, saved documents, and cross-session context central to the product.
- Treat CRM, outreach, and other vertical workflows as optional integrations. Do not replace the general workspace with a vertical-specific interface or positioning.
- Make saved work easy to discover and resume. Distinguish explicitly saved next actions from inferred progress or suggestions.
- Preserve existing configurations, data, MCP tools, checkpoints, and document revisions. Do not seed real deployments with synthetic personal records.
- Prefer additive changes and read-only views when improving discovery. Keep writes on the existing canonical paths and retain revision checks.
- Verify owner isolation, saved-work retrieval, and existing general-purpose workflows alongside any integration-specific behavior.
- Describe demonstrations accurately: scripted clients are not autonomous agents, reported interactions are not verified deliveries, and advisory checks do not authorize external actions.
